# FORMAT SPEC — *Ecology and Evolution* (Wiley) Research Article

Authoritative, checkable specification of the *Ecology and Evolution* research-article
format, derived **structurally** from the reference article (Zenato Lazzari et al. 2025,
*Ecology and Evolution* 15:e71465, DOI 10.1002/ece3.71465). This document describes
headings, ordering, and formatting conventions only. No body text/sentences are
reproduced from the source; heading labels quoted below are short generic section names.

Use this as the target when reviewing draft manuscripts for format fidelity. The
**Review Checklist** at the end is the scoring instrument.

---

## 1. Front matter (order is fixed)

Elements appear in this exact order at the top of page 1:

1. **Journal banner** — running journal name (`Ecology and Evolution`) at the very top.
2. **Article-type + access labels** — on one line, ALL CAPS, e.g. `RESEARCH ARTICLE` (left)
   and an access label such as `OPEN ACCESS` (right).
3. **Title** — bold, large, Title Case. May span two lines. Latin binomial in the title is
   italicized where it appears.
4. **Author list** — full given + family names in reading order. Affiliation keys are
   **superscript numerals** placed immediately after each name (a single author may carry
   several, e.g. `Name^{1,2}`). Authors are separated by a spaced vertical bar ` | `.
   The corresponding author is *not* specially marked in the byline; it is identified in
   the Correspondence block (item 6).
5. **Affiliations block** — a single running paragraph. Each affiliation is prefixed by a
   **superscript numeral** matching the byline, and affiliations are separated by a spaced
   vertical bar ` | `. Numbering is sequential from 1 in order of first appearance.
6. **Correspondence** — label `Correspondence:` followed by the corresponding author's name
   and email in parentheses, e.g. `Correspondence: Name (name@domain)`.
7. **History line** — `Received: <date> | Revised: <date> | Accepted: <date>`, dates spelled
   as `DD Month YYYY`, separated by spaced vertical bars.
8. **Funding** — label `Funding:` followed by a single sentence/short paragraph naming the
   funding bodies. (Distinct from Acknowledgements.)
9. **Keywords** — label `Keywords:` followed by terms separated by a **spaced vertical bar**
   ` | ` (NOT commas or semicolons). Lowercase terms. Reference article uses **6** keywords.
   Count band to enforce: **4–8** keywords.
10. **Abstract** — heading `ABSTRACT` (small caps / bold). **A single unstructured paragraph**
    (no sub-labels like Background/Methods). Reference length ≈ **251 words**; enforce a band
    of **~150–300 words**.
11. **License + copyright** — Creative Commons open-access statement and `© <year> The Author(s)`
    publisher line near the foot of page 1.
12. **Self-citation footer** — journal name, year, volume:e-locator and DOI at the bottom of
    page 1, e.g. `Ecology and Evolution, 2025; 15:e71465` / `https://doi.org/...`.

> **Bilingual note (source-specific, OPTIONAL):** the reference article additionally includes a
> second-language abstract (`RESUMO`) and a translated keywords line directly below the English
> ones. This is author/journal-permitted, not a required element. A monolingual draft is
> compliant without it; if present, it must mirror the English Abstract as a single paragraph.

## 2. Body

- **Layout:** two-column text.
- **IMRaD sequence (top-level, numbered):**
  1. `1 | Introduction`
  2. `2 | Materials and Methods`
  3. `3 | Results`
  4. `4 | Discussion`
- **Heading rendering / numbering convention (literal):**
  - Top-level: `N | Title` — an arabic section number, a **spaced vertical bar** ` | `, then the
    title in **Title Case**. Example literal form: `1 | Introduction`.
  - Second-level: `N.N | Title` — dotted two-part number, spaced vertical bar, Title Case title.
    Example literal form: `2.1 | Panel Design Using Whole-Genome Data`.
  - The separator between number and title is always the vertical bar `|` (with surrounding
    spaces), never a period or a tab. Numbering is continuous and hierarchical.
  - No deeper than second level is used in the reference article.

### Observed subsections under **Materials and Methods** (2)
- `2.1 | Panel Design Using Whole-Genome Data`
- `2.2 | Inter-Biome Differentiation Assessment`
- `2.3 | Informative SNP Selection`
- `2.4 | Assessment of SNP Panel Performance`
- `2.5 | Validation With Additional Genomic Data`

### Observed subsections under **Results** (3)
- `3.1 | Basic Population Genetic Statistics`
- `3.2 | Development and Assessment of the SNP Panel`
- `3.3 | Validation Using Additional Whole-Genome Sequences`

### Discussion (4)
- Single flowing section; **no numbered subsections** observed. (A draft may add `4.1 …`
  subsections and remain structurally valid, but the reference uses none.)

## 3. Back matter (end sections)

Unnumbered, bold, Title-Case headings. **Observed order** (from the two-column visual flow of
the reference article; page-12 image is ground truth — column-interleaving text extraction is
unreliable here):

1. **Author Contributions** — per-author roles using the CRediT taxonomy, with contribution
   levels in parentheses (e.g. `conceptualization (equal)`, `formal analysis (lead)`).
2. **Acknowledgements** — non-financial thanks + fellowship/institutional acknowledgements.
3. **Disclosure** — benefit-sharing statement (opens `Benefits generated:`). *(Common in
   EcolEvol; treat as recommended, not strictly required.)*
4. **Ethics Statement** — permits / sampling approvals.
5. **Conflicts of Interest** — declaration (e.g. "The authors declare no conflicts of interest.").
6. **Data Availability Statement** — repository/accession details (Dryad DOI, SRA BioProject, etc.).
7. **References**
8. **Supporting Information** — short pointer paragraph to the online SI section (appears after
   References, on the final page).

Notes:
- Heading label variants accepted by the journal: singular/plural (`Conflict(s) of Interest`,
  `Acknowledgement(s)`). Enforce presence, tolerate the variant.
- **`How to cite this article`** footnote: not present as extractable text in the reference file
  (may be rendered as image or added at typesetting). Treat as **OPTIONAL / typeset-added**, not
  a draft requirement.
- The exact ordering above is the reference's; the load-bearing rule for review is that all
  required end sections are **present** and that **References precedes Supporting Information**.
  Minor reordering among the statement blocks (Author Contributions / Ethics / Conflicts / Data
  Availability / Acknowledgements) is within journal tolerance.

## 4. Citations & references

### In-text citations — author–year (name–year)
- Parenthetical form has **no comma** between author and year: `(Surname et al. 2002)`.
- Multiple citations in one parenthesis are separated by **semicolons** and ordered
  **alphabetically**: `(Author A et al. 2019; Author B et al. 2021)`.
- Narrative/first mention uses `Surname (Year)` or `(Surname Year)`; `et al.` for 3+ authors.

### Reference list
- **Unnumbered, alphabetical** by first author's family name.
- Wiley author-year entry style:
  - **Journal article:** `Family, A., B. Family, and C. Family. Year. "Article Title in Title
    Case." Journal Name In Italics Volume, no. Issue: firstpage–lastpage. https://doi.org/...`
    — article title in **double quotation marks**, journal name **italicized**, `no.` for issue,
    en-dash page range, full DOI as an `https://doi.org/...` URL at the end.
  - **Book:** `Family, A. Year. Title In Italics. Edition (e.g. 2nd ed.). Publisher.`
  - **Book chapter:** `Family, A. Year. "Chapter Title." In Book Title In Italics, edited by
    Ed. Name, pages. Publisher. https://doi.org/...`
  - Author initials follow the family name for **all** authors (`Family, A., B. Family, ...`);
    the last author is preceded by `and`.
  - **4+ authors:** truncate with `et al.` after listing the first three (`Family, A., B.
    Family, C. Family, et al. Year. ...`).
  - **Dataset** entries are tagged `[dataset]`.

## 5. Figures & tables

- **Caption label format (literal):** `FIGURE N | <caption>` and `TABLE N | <caption>` — the
  word `FIGURE`/`TABLE` in **ALL CAPS**, the arabic number, a **spaced vertical bar** ` | `,
  then the caption sentence.
- **Numbering:** sequential in order of first in-text mention; figures and tables numbered in
  independent series (Figure 1…; Table 1…).
- **Placement:** table captions sit **above** the table; figure captions sit **below** the figure.
- **Sub-panels:** lower-case parenthetical labels `(a)`, `(b)`, … referenced in the caption.
- **In-text references:** `Figure N`, `Table N` (capitalized, spelled out).
- **Supplementary items:** S-prefixed (`Table S1`, `Figure S1`), pointed to via the Supporting
  Information section.

## 6. REVIEW CHECKLIST (pass/fail per item)

**Front matter**
- [ ] Article-type label present and ALL CAPS (e.g. `RESEARCH ARTICLE`).
- [ ] Title in Title Case; Latin binomials italicized.
- [ ] Author byline uses full names, superscript affiliation numerals, ` | ` separators.
- [ ] Affiliations block: superscript-numbered, sequential from 1, ` | ` separated, matches byline keys.
- [ ] `Correspondence:` block present with name + email; corresponding author identifiable.
- [ ] `Received | Revised | Accepted` history line present with `DD Month YYYY` dates.
- [ ] `Funding:` statement present and distinct from Acknowledgements.
- [ ] `Keywords:` present, **vertical-bar separated**, 4–8 terms, lowercase.
- [ ] Abstract is a **single unstructured paragraph**, ~150–300 words.

**Body**
- [ ] Top-level headings numbered `N | Title` with the ` | ` separator, Title Case.
- [ ] Second-level headings numbered `N.N | Title`, ` | ` separator, Title Case.
- [ ] IMRaD order: Introduction → Materials and Methods → Results → Discussion.
- [ ] Materials and Methods is broken into logical numbered subsections (`2.1…`).
- [ ] Results is broken into logical numbered subsections (`3.1…`).
- [ ] No orphaned/skipped section numbers; numbering is continuous and hierarchical.

**Back matter**
- [ ] Author Contributions present (CRediT roles with contribution levels).
- [ ] Ethics Statement present (if human/animal/sample permits apply).
- [ ] Conflict(s) of Interest declaration present.
- [ ] Data Availability Statement present (repository/accession identifiers).
- [ ] Acknowledgements present.
- [ ] References present and **precede** Supporting Information.
- [ ] Supporting Information pointer present (if SI exists).
- [ ] End-section headings are unnumbered, bold, Title Case.

**Citations & references**
- [ ] In-text citations are author–year with **no comma** before the year.
- [ ] Multiple in-text citations semicolon-separated and alphabetical.
- [ ] Reference list alphabetical, unnumbered.
- [ ] Journal entries: quoted article title, italic journal, `Vol, no. Issue: pages`, trailing DOI URL.
- [ ] `et al.` truncation after three authors for 4+ author works.

**Figures & tables**
- [ ] Captions use `FIGURE N | …` / `TABLE N | …` (ALL-CAPS label + ` | ` separator).
- [ ] Figures and tables each numbered sequentially by first mention.
- [ ] Table captions above tables; figure captions below figures.
- [ ] Sub-panels labeled `(a)`, `(b)` and referenced in the caption.
- [ ] Supplementary items use S-prefix (`Table S1`, `Figure S1`).
